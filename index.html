PRIMARY RESEARCH

Thin-Film Fracture & Curvature Studies

My research focuses on understanding the mechanics of thin films and interfaces under peeling, bending and curvature-driven loading.

The work combines experimental characterization with nonlinear finite element analysis and cohesive-zone modeling to investigate interfacial crack initiation, propagation and damage evolution.
